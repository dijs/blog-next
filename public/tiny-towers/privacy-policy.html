Privacy Policy

Tiny Towers · Effective August 24, 2026

Overview

Tiny Towers is developed by Richard van der Dys. Tiny Towers does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information.

Information collection

Tiny Towers does not require an account and does not contain advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies. The app does not collect names, email addresses, contact information, location, device identifiers, gameplay activity, or diagnostic data.

Game progress

Your wave record, kingdom generations, unlocked features, Kingdom Laws, Chronicle progress, Muster choices, Crown Trials, and related preferences are saved locally on your device. Tiny Towers does not upload this game progress to a developer-operated server.

Deleting the app may remove its locally saved progress. Device backups and restoration are controlled by you, your device settings, and Apple rather than by Tiny Towers.

Device permissions

Tiny Towers does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location. Core gameplay does not require a Tiny Towers account or a remote cloud service.

Children’s privacy

Because Tiny Towers does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Apple and platform services

Apple may process information independently when you download, update, back up, or otherwise use the app through Apple services. That processing is governed by Apple’s own terms and privacy policies. Tiny Towers does not receive personal information from those services through the app.

Security and retention

Tiny Towers does not operate a user database or retain personal data on developer-controlled servers. Your game progress remains subject to the security, storage, and backup settings of your device.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Tiny Towers’ features or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to richard.vanderdys@gmail.com.
